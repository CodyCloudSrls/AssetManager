# NIS2 Pack Audit

Last updated: 2026-05-14

This audit covers the configured NIS2 framework packs generated by `config/compliance_frameworks.php` through `App\Support\Compliance\ComplianceFrameworkPackCatalog`.

The audit conclusion is deliberately narrow: `nis2_it` remains the only shipped national overlay. Every other NIS2 pack is an EU baseline starter pack and must not be presented as country-specific legal content.

## Italy Overlay Review

`nis2_it` is aligned as an operational starter pack, not as final legal advice.

Verified legal and operational anchors:

- `D.Lgs. 138/2024`: registered as the national transposition source.
- ACN update/register guidance: registered as the operational authority source for Italian NIS information updates.
- European Commission Italy page: records Italy as transposed and identifies ACN as single point of contact.
- EU NIS2 Directive: retained as the baseline source for harmonized governance, risk, incident, continuity and supply-chain requirements.

Supplier and relevant-data coverage:

- Supplier relevance, criticality, assessment state, rationale and review dates are covered by supplier NIS2 fields.
- Supplier tax code/Codice IPA, country and legal name are available for the ACN-oriented supplier export.
- CPV coverage is handled through supplier CPV fields and the CPV catalog/search flow.
- Contact data remains on supplier records and linked evidence; the ACN ODS export intentionally exports only the columns present in `docs/ACN_Template_fornitori.ods`.
- Requirement `NIS2-SUP-02` points supplier evidence to CPV, contracts, DPA/SLA, questionnaires, attestations, notifications and remediation plans.

Open legal risk:

- ACN pages and operational templates can change outside this repository. Before a consultant submits or certifies Italian NIS data, the ACN source and template must be rechecked manually.

## Non-Italian National Review

The country matrix was reviewed on 2026-05-14 using the Commission transposition index as the official EU-level starting point. The presence of a country page is not enough to ship a national overlay because this product has not completed country-law extraction, translation, expert review and client applicability review for those jurisdictions.

Current decision:

- `EU`: `baseline_only`, uses `nis2_eu`.
- `IT`: `implemented`, uses `nis2_it`.
- `AT`, `BE`, `BG`, `CY`, `CZ`, `DE`, `DK`, `EE`, `ES`, `FI`, `FR`, `GR`, `HR`, `HU`, `IE`, `LT`, `LU`, `LV`, `MT`, `NL`, `PL`, `PT`, `RO`, `SE`, `SI`, `SK`: `review_required`, no national pack key, fallback `nis2_eu`.

## Audit Result

No NIS2 pack content change is required beyond the 2026-05-14 source-register refresh and pack version bump.

Required operational controls remain:

- Do not mass-update tenant data.
- Update system templates first through the superadmin pack console.
- Apply tenant-safe updates only to selected tenants after diff review.
- Treat all tenant copies as editable operational records.
- Keep non-Italian packs visibly EU-baseline until a country-specific expert review is recorded.
