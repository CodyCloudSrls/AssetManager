# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant principle of respectful, professional collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- be direct and technically honest
- disagree on ideas without attacking people
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and doxxing
- do not publish secrets, customer data, or private infrastructure details

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to `security@codycloud.it` until a dedicated community address is published.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and discussions that violate this policy.
